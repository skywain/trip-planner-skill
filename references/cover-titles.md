# Cover titles — bilingual poetic case library 封面标题案例库

For rendered deliverables (HTML cover pages, album covers). Pick, adapt, or use as a
quality bar for new coinages. Rules:

- **zh**: 2-6 characters, must survive as a huge serif display word. **en**: 1-5
  elegant words — a companion line, not a translation exercise. (Non-Chinese trip?
  The field roles and Latin budgets are in the section after the table.)
- Cite allusions honestly: classical/public-domain sources may be quoted; in-copyright
  contemporary prose may *inspire* but never be quoted. Mark original coinages 原创化用.
- Subtitle slot: the source line (原句) or the route list. A small credit (—— 作者)
  under the English line is the classiest way to explain an allusion.
- **Cliché blacklist** (never use): 说走就走 / 诗与远方 / 在路上(直接用) / 山在召唤 /
  远方的田野 / Wanderlust / Life is a journey.

## Non-Chinese trips — same fields, Latin budgets

The table above is a Chinese canon; for an English (or any Latin-script) trip use it
as **method**, not menu — an allusion, a season word, a colour word, a place-scale
word — and fill the same art fields (settled after the London→Japan test, F9):

- **Field roles do not change** (`themes.<theme>.cover`, see ART-SCHEMA): `zh` = the
  big display title (h1) — **it may be an English phrase**, the key name is
  historical; `sub` = the copy line under it (route list or the quoted source line);
  `en` = the CAPS English line, a companion not a translation — when `zh` is already
  English, make `en` a longer second line, not a repeat; `credit` = the source, small
  (`— after Bashō, public domain` / `original coinage`); `kick` / `kick_en` stay the
  short trip word (`Japan 2026` / `JAPAN 2026`).
- **Latin character budgets per theme** (the renderers were sized for 2-6 CJK
  glyphs; these are the measured or declared ceilings, spaces included):
  - 插画 illustrated `h1` ≈ **11 characters** — "Late Maples" fills the 500 px line
    edge to edge; 12+ wraps or shrinks;
  - 手账 journal `h1` **auto-sizes in 4 tiers** by weighted length (a CJK glyph
    counts 1, a Latin letter/digit 0.6): ≤5 Latin chars = full size, ≤7 one notch
    down, ≤10 smaller, anything longer shrinks to fit the width in one line and
    never wraps — so ~10 Latin characters is where it stops looking like a title;
  - zine `days[].theme` (vertical `.vtitle`) **≤ 10 characters** — English letters
    stack upright and read as decoration; longer than 10 turns into a 1,400 px
    column (override per day via `themes.zine.days[d].theme`); zine **`cover.zh` is
    a vertical column of 2-4 glyphs** (`clamp(96px,19vw,196px)` per glyph — 4 CJK
    characters are already ~784 px tall on desktop; a 5th glyph or a Latin word
    longer than 4 letters clips off the page — 人海 / KOYO are the right size);
  - 夜航 noir `h1` (`.lead h1`, `clamp(56px,12vw,168px)`, `.1em` tracking, full
    viewport width minus the gutter, and it **wraps** rather than shrinks):
    **≤ 10 characters conservative** — mobile 375 px is the binding case (~9-10
    chars at the 56 px floor); the Mexico test shipped "Night Vigil" (11) and it
    rendered, desktop-probed — verify with `xprobe.sh … module` at both widths
    before shipping longer;
  - 黏土 clay `h1`: with a title sticker the budget belongs to the **image** — the
    sticker is `min(420px, 72vw)` wide, so ~**12 characters** with the last letter
    given room (the "QINC" lesson below); the text fallback (`clamp(40px,8vw,84px)`,
    weight 900, `.1em`) fits ~12 at 375 px and wraps beyond — "≤12, verify with
    xprobe" either way;
  - 玻璃 glass `h1`: **≤ 10 characters measured** (the Morocco test: "MOON OF QIN" =
    11 wraps over the hero plate; "Ochre Road" = 10 is the safe example) — 闪屏
    splash `h1`: **≤ 12 characters** placeholder until the builders publish a
    measured value (title board); on either, a longer phrase goes to `sub`;
  - `en` (CAPS line) — ≤ ~32 characters on every theme; `sub` — one route line
    ≤ ~40 characters or two lines with `\n`.
- **Two worked English examples**:
  - `zh: "Late Maples"` · `en: "EIGHT DAYS IN THE RED SEASON"` · `sub: "Tokyo →
    Hakone → Kyoto · 21-28 Nov"` · `credit: "after Bashō — public domain source;
    title is an original coinage"` (Japan koyo trip; the season word does the work
    the colour word 碧/苍 does in the table).
  - `zh: "MOON OF QIN"` · `en: "XI'AN AND BEIJING, EIGHT NIGHTS"` · `sub: "秦时明月汉时关
    — 王昌龄"` · `credit: "Wang Changling, 8th c. — public domain"` (China trip; the
    source line lives in `sub` and stays Chinese — bilingual by design, not by
    accident. Note the clay sticker put its crescent right after "QIN" and read
    "QINC": when a title is baked into an image, leave the last letter breathing
    room. And 11 characters is **one too many for glass** — it wrapped over the
    hero plate in the Morocco test; that trip's `zh: "Ochre Road"` (10) · `en:
    "MARRAKECH TO THE BLUE MOUNTAIN"` · `credit: "original coinage · Ibn Battuta set
    out from Tangier, 1325"` is the glass-safe shape.)
- **Cite honestly in English too**: a public-domain original line (Bashō, Li Bai,
  Whitman, Muir) may be quoted and is marked `public domain`; a phrase you made up
  is marked `original coinage`; in-copyright prose may inspire but is never quoted.
  Same cliché blacklist applies (`Wanderlust`, `Life is a journey`, `Eat Pray…`).

**Worked example** (US 2026: 北京→纽约→哥伦布球赛→黄石→优胜美地→夏威夷火山→北京):
top pick 碧海苍梧(全程弧线总括:碧海=太平洋+夏威夷压轴,苍梧=黄石/优胜美地远山),
备选 江入大荒(横贯大陆段的镜头语言)、落日熔金(火山压轴点题+自带配色)。封面排布:
eyebrow 日期 → h1 碧海苍梧 → EN 字距行 DAWN SEAS · DUSK PEAKS → 引线「朝碧海而暮苍梧
—— 徐霞客」→ 路线副题。
